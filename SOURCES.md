## Borough council: Extraordinary Council meeting, 16 November 2026

These are **primary sources**: the council's own published documents. They are listed by the names used on the council's "Agenda and draft minutes" page for the meeting. Find them from the council's [agendas, reports and minutes index](https://www.west-norfolk.gov.uk/meetings) by choosing Council, then Monday 16th November 2026. They are not copied into this repository.

| Document (as named by the council) | What it confirms |
|------------------------------------|------------------|
| Agenda frontsheet | The meeting was called for one substantive item, NSIPs (agenda item 5) |
| Agenda reports pack | Contains the item 5 report and the NSIP presentation slides |
| Report: "Nationally Significant Infrastructure Projects (NSIPs): Overview of the Development Consent Order Process and the Council's Role" (item C:77) | Statutory pre-application consultation was removed earlier in 2026. Interested parties register by submitting relevant representations at the pre-examination stage. The council need not take a formal position at pre-application |
| NSIP Presentation | Lists other NSIPs in or affecting the borough, including The Droves Solar Farm, Fens Reservoir, Redmere Wind Farm and Medworth Energy from Waste. Shows Longmarsh at the scoping stage |
| Summary of Public Questions and Responses | "The Council has not reached a formal position on the proposal" |
| Printed draft minutes | Item C:77: the general five-point NSIP resolution, carried unanimously. Note that these are **draft** minutes until the council approves them |
