## What is the proposal?

Longmarsh Green Energy is a proposed Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project (NSIP) in West Norfolk by EPL Norfolk Limited. It has two parts:
- a **Western Site** near Tilney St Lawrence, with the data centre, solar panels and batteries;
- an **Eastern Site** near Fincham and Barton Bendish, with solar panels and batteries.

The two sites are linked to Walpole substation by overhead and underground lines. The scheme combines up to 1.3 GW of solar and battery storage with a 1 GW data centre on about 4,700 acres of farmland.

[Check before quoting: as quoted in the campaign's Schedule A, the developer's proposals page describes solar panels and battery containers up to 4.5 metres high. Its October 2026 Scoping Report describes data-centre buildings up to 25 metres including plant.]

Ministers decide NSIPs, not the borough council. The application goes through the Planning Inspectorate.

## What this consultation is, and what comes next

- **This is a pre-application consultation run by the developer.** It is not the formal planning examination.
- **Statutory pre-application consultation was removed earlier in 2026.** The borough council's 16 November 2026 report says "the removal of Statutory Consultation earlier this year" has shortened the pre-application phase for some projects. The developer may therefore not be legally required to answer each comment individually. Ask for answers anyway, but don't assume you'll get them.
- **The formal stage comes later.** Once the Planning Inspectorate accepts the application, anyone can register as an **interested party** by submitting a **relevant representation** on the registration form on the project's page. The registration period is usually at least 28 days, or 30 days for projects needing an Environmental Impact Assessment. If you want a say in the examination, don't miss that window.

Check before relying on this. The official guidance is here:
- [Planning Act 2008: guidance on the pre-examination stage of an application](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/planning-act-2008-guidance-on-the-pre-examination-stage-of-an-application) (gov.uk) covers registering as an interested party and making a relevant representation.
- [Planning Act 2008: guidance on the examination stage of an application](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/planning-act-2008-guidance-on-the-examination-stage-of-an-application) (gov.uk).

## The borough council's position

On 16 November 2026, King's Lynn and West Norfolk Borough Council unanimously passed a **general five-point resolution on NSIPs** (minutes item C:77). It is not a vote against Longmarsh. In brief, the council will:
1. work with other Norfolk councils for a more strategic and locally accountable approach to NSIPs;
2. write to the Energy Secretary pressing for reforms;
3. where appropriate, oppose renewable energy developments that lack local support, threaten productive farmland and wildlife, or give no direct local benefit;
4. call for a review of energy distribution and infrastructure policy;
5. evaluate the Secretary of State's final decisions, and challenge them through the courts if warranted, with a budget for this.

The council has said it **"has not reached a formal position on the proposal"**. At the meeting, the Monitoring Officer said the council was not deciding on any individual proposal. See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) for the council documents.

## Important notices

> **These are templates to adapt, not legal advice.** Check that facts, figures and policy references are accurate and apply to you before using them.

> **Policy wording:** check references to the NPPF, EN-1, EN-3 and other policy documents against the current published versions before quoting. Policy is updated and paragraph numbers change.

> **Personal details:** our quick-start files contain no real names, addresses or contact details, only placeholders. The campaign's letters are its shareable versions: the sender's name is replaced with `xxxx`, but the village is the campaign author's. The letter to ministers lists public office email addresses for ministers, MPs and councillors, as given in the campaign's template.

> **Agricultural land:** if you are a landowner, tenant farmer or otherwise have an interest in the land, get independent professional advice.
