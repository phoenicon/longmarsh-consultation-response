**Name:** [your name]
**Address / Postcode:** [your address]

I am asking EPL Norfolk Limited / Longmarsh Green Energy to answer the following questions as part of its consultation report and in its development consent order (DCO) application.

---

**Agricultural land and food security**

1. What share of the approximately 1,900 hectares is grade 1, 2 and 3a agricultural land, by individual parcel? Please publish the parcel-level Agricultural Land Classification survey.

2. Which brownfield, previously developed and lower-grade land sites were assessed during site selection, against what criteria, and why was each rejected?

**The data centre**

3. Who will occupy the data centre, and when will that be made public?

4. When will visuals, photomontages and a landscape assessment be published? Please confirm the maximum heights of the solar panels, battery containers and data-centre buildings, including plant and any raised ground. [Check before quoting: the Stop Longmarsh campaign's Schedule A quotes the developer's figures as panels up to 4.5 m, battery containers up to 4.5 m, and data-centre buildings up to 25 m including plant.] How will the scheme affect nearby protected sites and heritage, and why are the proposed buffers enough? [Check before quoting: reported to include Islington Heronry SSSI, with a 100 m buffer, Birch Wood ancient woodland and the Fincham Conservation Area.]

5. What share of the solar output will serve the data centre, and what share will go to the grid? Please publish an hourly energy model.

**Water, cooling and noise**

6. What is the cooling architecture and final heat rejection method? What are the expected annual and peak water consumption figures, including during summer heatwaves, and what is the water source? How will surface-water runoff and flood risk across the Fenland drainage network be managed? [Check before quoting: parts of the site are reported to lie in Flood Zone 3.]

7. What are the forecast noise levels at the nearest homes, day and night — including low-frequency and tonal noise — and why is 300 metres the chosen separation distance for the data centre?

**Compulsory purchase and transmission infrastructure**

8. Is compulsory acquisition being sought through the DCO, over which land and rights, and how many farm businesses would lose all or part of their holding?

9. Was undergrounding of the full transmission route considered, and what would it cost?

**Scheme structure**

10. Why are the solar development and the data centre combined as a single application? What would happen to the solar scheme if the data centre did not go ahead?

**Long-term land use**

11. The developer's proposals state that after solar decommissioning in approximately 60 years, the Western Site data centre, substation, battery storage and cable route will remain permanently. What is the annual food production lost during those 60 years, and what is the independently costed decommissioning and land restoration plan — including who pays and when?

**Community benefit**

12. What community benefit is proposed, and how does it compare proportionately with the scale and 60-year operational lifetime of the development? What legally binding mechanisms will secure it?
